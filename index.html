How we made the images
Keeping the face consistent
We generated clothing edits and empty rooms separately. We then aligned each clothing edit with its source portrait, restored the source face, and placed the person over each room. A pixel check confirms that the central face region stays identical across all 16 versions of a person.
We divided the initial clothing and room assignments evenly between two image editors. Some images needed further editing to improve their appearance or make the intended change clearer. The downloadable records show those repairs and which editor made them.
Reading the examples
Each person's 4 × 4 grid offers a way to explore the method. The paper describes the separate private stimulus sets used in its analyses and gives their experimental counts. We have not collected human ratings of this synthetic collection. The original character briefs record artistic intentions; their impression scores have no psychometric validation.
The pixel checks establish consistency in the face region. Realism and the impressions these images evoke still need human evaluation. Two changes remain less clear than intended: S03's evening outfit and the clutter in S07's room.
