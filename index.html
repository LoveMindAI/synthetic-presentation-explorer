What this companion does—and does not—show
Controlled construction
Clothing edits and empty-room images were generated separately. The foreground was aligned to its source face and composited over the room plates. A fixed face-core mask checks identical pixels across each identity’s matrix.
Two image editors were balanced in the initial layer assignment. Subsequent documented repairs addressed visual and cue issues; the initial balance is not a claim that every final repair used the initially assigned editor.
Interpretation boundary
This is an illustrative 4 × 4 matrix, not a replacement for the study’s private stimulus sets or their experimental counts. No new human response data accompany these synthetic images. Original intended impression scores are artistic targets, not validated psychometric profiles.
Pixel preservation does not establish naturalism or psychological validity. Two moderate or ambiguous cues remain: S03’s evening outfit and S07’s cluttered room.
